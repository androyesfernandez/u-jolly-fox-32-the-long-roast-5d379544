# Duel tournament notes — The Long Roast

Two rival drafters each wrote ten candidate roast lines, so twenty lines
entered the tournament. Every pair was judged both ways round with the kit's
own brief as the judge, and the scores were averaged across both directions.

The winner was entry B6: the story of Andro spending a night repacking a
33-byte loader to dodge a duplicate check, only for the server to reject it
anyway — capped by the size joke that his code is smaller than his excuses
and his excuses fit in one line. Its duel mean was 0.65, clearing the 0.60
bar set for the tournament.

Runner-up was B2 at 182 characters, the line about submitting the loser
anyway with a straight face and a changelog.

A rival critic attacked the winning draft before it was frozen: the punch
arrived too late, and two dead words ("once" and "whole") were dragging the
middle. The fixes cut both words and tightened the ending from "are one
line long" to "fit in one line". The final version is 181 characters.
